# 合规和风险检查

## Always Check

Flag or soften claims involving:

- Guaranteed income, “当天出单”, “稳定月入”, “躺赚”.
- Platform manipulation, fake orders, fake reviews, automation that violates rules.
- Counterfeit, prohibited, gray-market, medical, health, finance, education credential, or regulated goods.
- Claims that require proof:销量,利润,案例,成功率,排名,官方政策.

## Rewrite Rules

- Replace guaranteed outcomes with conditional/testing language.
- Keep the scene-based packaging shape while removing unsupported certainty. When “当天出单、节省百分比、利润倍数” lacks target-owned evidence, preview a verified executable action, a qualitative time-saving scene, or leave a proof placeholder; do not silently keep the numeric promise.
- Replace “一定能赚” with “适合小成本测试”.
- Replace fake or abusive platform tactics with compliant account setup, genuine listing quality, customer service, and data tracking.
- For health/finance/education topics, add boundaries: not professional advice, no guaranteed results, comply with platform rules.
- Do not create scripts that instruct users to sell counterfeit goods, evade enforcement, fake reviews/orders, or deceive buyers.

## Risk Output

Keep the detailed audit outside the audience-facing script. In the script, retain only a red line that changes whether the step can be executed. At the end of the deliverable, include `风险替换` when any claim was changed:

- `原方向`: what was risky.
- `替换为`: safer wording.
- `还缺`: proof or screenshot needed.
